The right provider. Or the right solution. Backed by evidence.

Skyclar Data & Matching connects two things that are usually separate: a data set of more than 100 nearshore, BPO and software providers audited in detail across Europe — and a matching that brings your need together with the right provider or solution, with evidence.

The problem

Outsourced. Disappointed. Brought back in-house.

We see it all the time: volume moves nearshore, total cost rises, quality disappoints — and everything comes back. Usually the mistake was not the model but the selection: made on slides and day rates instead of data.
