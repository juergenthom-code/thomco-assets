Overview

Good decisions start with good data.

Whether customer service, sales, back office or software: the success of an outsourcing or technology project is decided at the selection stage. Choosing on data rather than presentations wins time, predictability and quality from day one.

Our base covers more than 100 nearshore, BPO and software providers analysed in detail across Europe — recorded on a single grid from on-site audits, interviews and metrics. Consulting, data insights and Glinsen Matching build on it.
