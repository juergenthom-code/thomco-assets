Idea

The right tool for each task.

Glinsen is not a platform for everything. Every product has one clear task: find the right provider, win meetings, become visible, invoice orders, answer enquiries, manage partners, source modules.

We select the most proven solutions for these tasks, adapt them and operate them. That way you use exactly what you need — as a service from Skyclar, for your own team or under your brand.

Frequently asked

Questions about Glinsen.

Where does the name come from?

Glinsen alludes to glinting — the star above the ı stands for it. We want our customers and partners to shine.

Can I use a single product?

Yes. Every product can be used on its own; combinations make sense where tasks are connected.

Who operates the products?

Skyclar — including setup, updates, monitoring and support.

Where is data processed?

Preferably in Switzerland and the EU. Details are set out in the data processing agreement and the privacy documentation for each product.
