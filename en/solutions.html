Overview

Every area delivers something tangible.

Skyclar works in six areas that share one thing: they start with a result that can be measured — a study, a shortlist, meetings in the calendar, answered enquiries, partners that sell, modules on site.

The areas can be commissioned individually or combined. Where software makes the difference, we use proven Glinsen products — set up and operated by us.

Frequently asked

Questions about the solutions.

Can I combine several areas?

Yes. Common combinations are Data and CX (provider selection plus AI agents) or Sales and Partnership (sales plus partner channel). We align goals and metrics together.

How do I find the right area?

Describe your goal to us. In the first conversation we place it and tell you openly whether and how we deliver it.

Do I need Glinsen products to work with Skyclar?

No. We use Glinsen products where they help. You can also use them yourself — but you do not have to.

Where does Skyclar work?

From Bottighofen in Switzerland, Germany and Austria and — with partners — in other European countries.

Which goal should we reach?

One conversation is enough to define it.
