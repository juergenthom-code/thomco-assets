Overview

The new first page is an answer.

More and more buying decisions begin with a question to an AI assistant: “Which provider suits us?” The answer names only a few. Generative Engine Optimization — GEO for short — makes sure yours is among them: with content, structure and sources that AI systems trust.

That includes a well-kept reputation on Google and a website that convinces people and machines alike. Glinsen Visibility brings all three together — measured by a monthly score and by the enquiries that follow.

Frequently asked

Questions about Glinsen Visibility.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization: preparing content, structure and sources so that AI assistants know your offer, describe it correctly and recommend it. GEO complements classic search engine optimisation.

Can you delete reviews?

Only Google can remove reviews. We help report reviews that breach Google’s policies or the law, and help make genuine voices of satisfied customers visible. Bought or fabricated reviews are not part of our offer.

How quickly does it work?

First changes often show after a few weeks; AI systems pick up new sources at different speeds. That is why we measure monthly.

Do I need a new website?

Not necessarily. Targeted changes to content and structure are often enough; we only recommend a new website when it pays off.
