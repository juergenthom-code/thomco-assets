Frequently asked

Questions from partners.

Which companies fit as sales partners?

Companies with their own customer access: agencies, consultants, contact centres, IT service firms and sales organisations.

Is there account protection?

Yes. Registered active customers are protected under the rules of the partner agreement.

How are delivery partners selected?

By the same criteria we apply for our customers: quality, stability, languages, management — checked in conversation and on site.

Does this fit you?

Send us two sentences about your company — we will come back with a meeting proposal.
