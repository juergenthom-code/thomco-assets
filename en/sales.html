Overview

From the first search hit to the invoice.

Today customers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google before they talk to a vendor. If you do not appear there, you are not asked. That is why Skyclar Sales starts with visibility — and only ends with the signed order.

In between lies what B2B sales is about: clean audiences, multi-step outreach, honest qualification and meetings in your team’s calendar. For service providers who want to deliver cleanly after the deal, Glinsen ERP adds the operations — from order to invoice.
