Glinsen Partner is the workspace for sales and white-label partners: register deals, manage customers, see commissions, access materials, check statements — all in one place, under your brand.

Overview

Clarity for both sides.

A partnership works when both sides always know where they stand: which customers are registered? Which contracts are running? Which commission is due? Glinsen Partner answers these questions at a glance.

The portal is part of Skyclar Partnership. Partners get access from onboarding — with materials, demo access and everything they need to sell.

Frequently asked

Questions about Glinsen Partner.

Who gets access to the portal?

All Skyclar Partnership partners — from onboarding, with access for their team.

How does account protection work?

Registered active customers are protected under the rules of the partner agreement; registration and confirmation are documented in the portal.

Do my customers see Skyclar?

In the white-label model the products appear under your brand. The details are set out in the partner agreement.

When is commission paid out?

According to the schedule agreed in the partner agreement; every statement can be viewed in the portal.
