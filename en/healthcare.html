Overview

From question to decision — in weeks, not quarters.

Marketing, medical affairs and market access need robust answers: how do physicians decide? Which channels work? Where do patients drop out? Skyclar Healthcare answers such questions with dedicated survey waves, qualitative interviews and programmes along the patient journey.

We combine clean field access in Switzerland and the DACH region with AI-assisted analysis. The result is not a slide deck full of raw data but a clear recommendation — with the numbers that support it.

Frequently asked

Questions about Skyclar Healthcare.

How many physicians can you reach?

It depends on specialty and region. For specialties in German-speaking Switzerland, samples of 25 to 50 are realistic; for primary care considerably more. We tell you up front what is feasible.

What does a survey cost?

The price depends on number of questions, sample size and target group. You receive a proposal with a clear scope and an agreed sample size.

How quickly are results available?

Short surveys up to n = 50 typically in around four weeks from questionnaire approval; larger studies according to an agreed plan.

Do you work outside Switzerland?

Yes. We run surveys and patient programmes across the DACH region and — with partners — in other European countries.

Which question should we answer?

Outline your project — we will come back with a concrete proposal that fits your budget.
