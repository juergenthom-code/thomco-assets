Overview

Selection with a system.

A good provider selection starts with a clear description of the need and ends with a decision you can defend internally. Glinsen Matching structures that path: capture the need, weight the criteria, compare with the base, justify the result.

The foundation is provider profiles from on-site audits, interviews and metrics — all recorded on the same grid and therefore directly comparable. Skyclar Data uses Glinsen Matching in every selection project.

Frequently asked

Questions about Glinsen Matching.

Which providers are in the base?

Nearshore, BPO and software providers across Europe, with a focus on customer service, sales and back office. The base grows with every audit and every project.

How are the profiles created?

From on-site visits, interviews with management and teams, and metrics — recorded on a single grid.

How is the ranking determined?

From the criteria you define and weight. Every score is documented and traceable.

Can I use Glinsen Matching myself?

Yes. For your own selection projects or as part of a consulting mandate — ask us about access.
