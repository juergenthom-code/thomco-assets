Overview

The supply chain in view.

A solar project has many moving parts: module type, quantities, prices, delivery dates, containers, site windows, documents. Glinsen Solar keeps them together — so procurement, project management and site work with the same information.

Skyclar Solar uses Glinsen Solar for every procurement. Buying-group partners see their enquiries, quotes and deliveries in the same workspace.

Frequently asked

Questions about Glinsen Solar.

Do I have to order through Skyclar Solar?

Glinsen Solar is designed around procurement through Skyclar Solar; we are happy to discuss other models.

Which documents are stored?

Datasheets, certificates, warranty terms, order confirmations and delivery notes per project.

Can several people work together?

Yes. Procurement, project management and site see the same information — with suitable permissions.

How do I get access?

With your first enquiry to Skyclar Solar or as a buying-group partner.
