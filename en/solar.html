Overview

Direct from the manufacturer. On time for installation.

In solar projects the supply chain decides schedule and margin. Skyclar Solar sources modules directly from leading manufacturers, confirms availability and delivery date in writing and coordinates deliveries in step with the site.

Beyond that we bring projects, capital and off-takers together — for installers, developers and investors in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

Frequently asked

Questions about Skyclar Solar.

Which manufacturers do you work with?

With leading module manufacturers. The quote names manufacturer, type and datasheet.

Our focus is project quantities; smaller quantities are pooled through the buying group.

Do you deliver to Switzerland?

Yes, on request. Terms and delivery conditions are stated in the quote.

How quickly do I receive a quote?
