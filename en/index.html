Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions.

What does outcome as a service mean?

We agree a measurable outcome up front — such as qualified meetings, a study or a provider selection — and deliver it as a service. Which people and which technology it takes is our job.

Which companies does Skyclar work for?

For mid-sized and large companies in Switzerland, Germany and Austria — and for partners who offer our products under their own brand.

What is Glinsen?

Glinsen is our product family: proven software for clearly defined tasks, each assigned to a Skyclar area and operated by us. You use exactly what you need.

How does a collaboration start?

With a thirty-minute conversation. After that you know whether and how we deliver your outcome — and on what terms.

Where is data processed?

Preferably in Switzerland and the EU. Details are set out in the data processing agreement and privacy documentation for each solution.
