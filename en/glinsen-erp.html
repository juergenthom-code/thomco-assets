ERP

From order to invoice.

Glinsen ERP is the system for service providers: orders, assignments, time, billing and reporting in one place — so that won orders become cleanly delivered and promptly invoiced work.

Request a demo Skyclar Sales

Area: Skyclar Sales

Overview

One system instead of many spreadsheets.

Growing service providers know the picture: orders in the inbox, schedules in spreadsheets, time on paper, invoices in accounting. Every handover costs time and margin. Glinsen ERP brings these steps together in one continuous flow.

Within Skyclar Sales, Glinsen ERP closes the chain: what sales wins is planned, delivered and invoiced in operations — and made visible in reporting.

Frequently asked

Questions about Glinsen ERP.

Which companies is Glinsen ERP suited to?

For service providers with orders, assignments and time — from service operations to technical trades and consulting.

Can existing data be migrated?

Yes. We migrate customers, terms and open orders during the introduction.

Is there a connection to accounting?

Invoices and postings can be passed on to common accounting systems; we settle the right connection in the project.

How long does the introduction take?

It depends on scope and data. We start with one unit and expand step by step — you receive the timeline with the proposal.
