Privacy

1. General

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. We treat personal data confidentially and in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and this privacy policy.

This policy describes which data is processed when you visit this website. The website deliberately does without cookies for analytics or advertising, without tracking tools and without embedded social media content.

3. Visiting the website and server log data

When you visit this website, technically necessary data is automatically recorded by the server or hosting provider: IP address, date and time of access, page requested, referrer URL, browser type and version, and operating system.

This data is needed solely to provide the website securely and without errors, and is not combined with other data sources. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in stable and secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Log data is deleted by the hosting provider after a short period.

4. Hosting and delivery

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA. Images, films, fonts and other files are delivered via the jsDelivr content delivery network, which uses a network of international CDN providers. In the process, your IP address is necessarily transmitted to the respective servers.

Transfers to the USA or other countries without an adequate level of data protection take place only on the basis of appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or — where the provider is certified — the EU-U.S. or Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

5. Cookies, analytics and social networks

This website does not set cookies for analytics, marketing or advertising purposes and does not use analytics or tracking services. No social network plug-ins are embedded.

6. Fonts and media

The fonts used are delivered together with the website. No connection is made to Google Fonts or other font services. Films play without sound and without any connection to video platforms such as YouTube.

7. Contacting us

If you contact us by email or phone, we process your details (such as name, company, email address, phone number and your request) in order to answer your enquiry and for follow-up questions.

The contact form on this website does not store any data. It opens your email program with a prepared message; we only receive your details once you send it. The legal basis is the handling of your enquiry or the initiation of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

8. Disclosure of data

We do not pass your personal data on to third parties unless this is necessary to handle your enquiry, we use carefully selected processors (for example for hosting and email), or we are legally obliged to do so.

9. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes or as required by statutory retention obligations. The data is then deleted.

10. Your rights

Within the scope of applicable data protection law, you have the right to information about the personal data we hold about you, to rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, to object to processing, and to receive or transfer your data. You can withdraw any consent given at any time with effect for the future.

To exercise these rights, contact info@thomco.ch. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority — in Switzerland with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU with the authority of your place of residence.

11. Data security

For security reasons, this website uses TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by “https://” in your browser’s address bar.

12. Changes

We may amend this privacy policy if the website or legal requirements change. The version published on this page applies.
