Overview

AI for volume. People for judgement.

Customers expect an answer — immediately, in their language and on their channel. Skyclar CX puts AI agents where enquiries recur: information, bookings, status questions, qualification. Where judgement is needed, a person takes over — with full context.

At the same time we make your teams stronger: a copilot suggests answers and summarises conversations, an analysis evaluates every conversation, and a trainer lets new staff practise before they speak to real customers.

Frequently asked

Questions about Skyclar CX.

Do customers notice they are talking to AI?

The agent speaks naturally and identifies itself as AI — as the EU AI Act requires. On request a person takes over at any time.

What happens with complex requests?

The agent hands over to your team — with a summary so nobody has to ask twice.

Which channels are possible?

Phone, WhatsApp, web chat, email and SMS; a video avatar on request. A conversation can switch channels without losing context.

How quickly is an agent live?

A first use case on one channel is typically ready within a few weeks; further channels follow step by step.

How reachable are you today?

Tell us channels, volumes and service hours — we propose a start with a clear metric.
