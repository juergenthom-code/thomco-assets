Overview

One system for pipeline and meetings.

Good B2B sales is craft: the right companies, the right people, a message that lands, and persistence across several channels. Glinsen Sales takes the legwork off your team — research, lists, drafts, timing — so more time is left for conversations.

Skyclar Sales runs Glinsen Sales for customers as a service. Companies with their own sales team use it directly — meetings and activities land in the CRM you already know.

Frequently asked

Questions about Glinsen Sales.

Does Glinsen Sales replace our CRM?

No. Glinsen Sales complements your CRM: meetings, contacts and activities are transferred there.

Does the AI write on its own?

The AI drafts. Approval, tone and conversation stay with people — your team or Skyclar Sales.

What about data protection?

Outreach follows the GDPR, the Swiss FADP and competition law; data sources and opt-outs are documented.

Which languages are possible?

German, French, Italian and English; further languages on request.
