Outcomes under your brand.

Skyclar Partnership brings proven solutions as Glinsen products to partners who sell them under their own name — with onboarding, partner portal, operations and billing. We want our partners to shine.

Become a partner Glinsen products
Overview

Your name up front. Our operations behind it.

Many companies own the customer relationship but no technology of their own: agencies, consultants, contact centres, IT service firms, sales organisations. With Skyclar Partnership they offer AI agents, sales software or visibility under their own brand — without developing anything themselves.

Skyclar provides product, setup, support and billing. Partners focus on advice, sales and the relationship with their customers — and set their own prices.

Frequently asked

Yes. You sell under your own brand and at your own prices.
