Dialog

AI agents for every channel.

Glinsen Dialog answers enquiries by phone, WhatsApp, chat and email — around the clock and in natural language. Plus a copilot for your teams, analysis of every conversation and a trainer for new staff.

Request a demo Skyclar CX

Area: Skyclar CX

Overview

One conversation. Every channel. Always with context.

Customers choose whichever channel suits them — the phone in the morning, WhatsApp in the evening, email the next day. Glinsen Dialog treats these contacts as one conversation: the agent knows the history, answers questions, books appointments and hands over to people when judgement is needed.

Four building blocks turn this into complete customer service: Agent, Assist, Analyze and Coach. Skyclar CX sets them up, connects them to your systems and improves them continuously.

Building blocks

Agent, Assist, Analyze, Coach.

Usable on their own, strongest together.

Dialog Agent

The AI agent for service and sales

  • Phone, WhatsApp, web chat, email and SMS
  • Answers, qualifies, books appointments, creates tickets
  • Around the clock, without waiting
  • Warm handover to your team with a summary
Dialog Assist

The copilot for your staff

  • Answer suggestions from your systems and knowledge sources
  • Automatic conversation summaries
  • Live translation during the conversation
  • Shorter handling times, less after-call work
Dialog Analyze

Every conversation analysed

  • All conversations instead of samples
  • Sentiment, topics and trends across all channels
  • Early signals of complaints and churn
  • Forecast of customer satisfaction (CSAT, NPS)
Dialog Coach

Train before it counts

  • Realistic customer simulations
  • Feedback on the exact moment in the conversation
  • Practise without risk to real customers
  • Overview for team leads

Frequently asked

Questions about Glinsen Dialog.

How natural does the agent sound?

The agent speaks in natural language and in a voice that suits your brand. It identifies itself as AI.

What happens when the agent does not know?

It hands over to your team — with a summary — or schedules a callback. You set the rules.

Do we need all four building blocks?

No. Many customers start with the agent on one channel and add Assist, Analyze or Coach later.

Can partners resell Glinsen Dialog?

Yes. Through Skyclar Partnership, agencies, contact centres and IT service firms offer Glinsen Dialog under their own brand.
